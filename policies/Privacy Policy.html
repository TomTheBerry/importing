7. Cookies

Our control panel uses a small number of cookies:

  • Session cookie — keeps you logged in as you move between pages. Strictly necessary.
  • Security (CSRF) cookie — protects your account from forged requests. Strictly necessary.
  • "Remember me" cookie — keeps you logged in between visits when you log in. Strictly necessary for that feature.
  • Google reCAPTCHA — on the login and sign-up pages, Google may set or read cookies to tell people from bots.
  • Google, GitHub and Discord sign-in pages — if you choose to log in with or connect one of these, you are taken to their own website, where their cookies and privacy policies apply. We do not set any cookies for them.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Because the cookies we set are strictly necessary for the service to work, we do not ask for consent before setting them. Blocking them will stop you from logging in.

8. How we protect it

  • Passwords and recovery codes are stored only as one-way hashes; authenticator secrets and API key secrets are encrypted.
  • Two-step verification is available to every customer and required for all of our staff.
  • Card details are handled entirely by Stripe and never touch our servers.
  • Connections to our website and panel are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Staff access is limited by role, and every staff action — including opening a customer's server — is recorded.
  • Logins are rate-limited, and repeated failed attempts are temporarily blocked.
  • We email you when your account is logged into from a new device or network, and you can see and log out every device from Account → Security.

No system is completely secure. If a breach affects your information in a way that puts you at high risk, we will tell you without undue delay, and we will report it to the ICO where required.

9. Staff access to your servers

To give support, fix problems or investigate abuse, some of our staff can access your servers and account. We only do this when needed, and every access is logged with the staff member's name and the time. You can see your own account and server activity in the control panel.

Viewing the panel as you. To help with a support request, a senior administrator can view the control panel exactly as you see it. They must record a reason, it ends automatically after one hour, and everything they do is logged against their own name as well as yours. While doing this they cannot change your password, email address, two-step verification, payment details or connected accounts, agree to terms for you, or make data or deletion requests.

Staff notes. Staff may keep short, factual notes on your account to help with support. They are never shared outside our staff, and are included if you ask for a copy of your data.

Support tickets. Staff who handle support can read your tickets and their attached files, and may add internal notes to them. Tickets, including internal notes about them, are included if you ask for a copy of your data.

10. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • Access — get a copy of the personal information we hold about you. Ask from Account → Privacy in the control panel (or by email). We will reply within one month. If your request is complex we may extend this by up to two more months, and will tell you why within the first month. Your copy is a ZIP file you can download from your account for 7 days.
  • Rectification — have inaccurate information corrected. You can update most details yourself in your account settings.
  • Erasure — delete your account from Account → Privacy (or ask us by email). For your protection, your account is deleted 14 days later; logging in before then cancels it, and we email you either way. When it is deleted we remove your account, servers (including their files and backups), security settings, connected accounts and account credit, end your services so you are not charged again, and ask Stripe to delete your saved card. Your support tickets and their files are deleted too. We keep invoices and payment records, with your name, for the period in section 6, and security logs are deleted automatically within 90 days. Staff accounts must have their staff access removed first.
  • Restriction — ask us to limit how we use your information while a concern is looked into.
  • Objection — object to uses based on legitimate interests, and to any direct marketing at any time.
  • Portability — the copy of your data is in JSON, a common machine-readable format you can take elsewhere.
  • Withdraw consent — where we rely on consent, at any time.
  • Disconnect accounts — remove a connected Google, GitHub or Discord account at any time from Account → Connections. We delete what we stored about it straight away.
  • Remove your date of birth — at any time from your Account page.

The quickest way to get a copy of your data or delete your account is from Account → Privacy in the control panel. For anything else, or if you can no longer log in, email privacy@theberryhost.com from the email address on your account. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request. We will reply within one month, and there is normally no charge.
